- Chiudi chiedendo un controllo di unità e ordine di grandezza.

English:
How to let AI guide you through a physics problem without handing you the solution: a prompt with a role, rules and a final check. Real test with Claude.

Theory recap:
- A good prompt sets a role (tutor), rules (one question at a time) and what NOT to do (no formulas or results).
- If you get it wrong, ask for a hint, not the correction.
- Finish by asking for a check of units and order of magnitude.
- The result is yours only if you worked it out yourself.
